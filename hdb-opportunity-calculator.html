Which HDB option offers better long-term value?

Compare a new 2-room BTO with 3- or 4-room resale flats as both homes and investments. Explore possible flat price growth and room rent, alongside the cash you could invest in equities. These are uncertain scenarios; the calculator keeps the factors separate and does not declare one overall investment winner.

New to these terms? See a few quick definitions
BTO
A new flat bought directly from HDB. You apply before it is built and wait for the keys.
Resale
An existing flat bought from its current owner.
CPF OA
Your CPF Ordinary Account savings. Some can be used for a home, subject to CPF rules.
Equity investments
Shares in companies or funds. Their value can rise or fall; the example return is not guaranteed.
MSR
HDB's limit on how large a monthly housing-loan payment can be compared with income.
Assessment rate
A higher interest rate used to estimate the loan size. It is not the rate used for the regular payment estimate.
Minimum stay
The period a buyer generally must live in the flat before selling it. HDB rules depend on flat type.
Buyer’s Stamp Duty
A tax paid when buying a home. The estimate uses current rates.
Money left / short
A rough check of upfront purchase money against the cash and CPF OA amounts entered above.

How the estimates are worked out

Definitions and formulas used in the calculator outputs
What you see In plain language What it means
Estimated loan and monthly paymentThe estimated loan is the lower of the selected share of the flat price and the loan amount supported by the monthly payment limit. Monthly payments depend on the loan, interest rate, and years to repay it.HDB decides the real loan amount. The assessment rate estimates loan size; the other rate estimates regular monthly payments.
Money needed at purchaseFlat price minus estimated loan and any grant, plus Buyer Stamp Duty.Legal fees, valuation shortfall, renovation, furnishing, and moving costs are not included.
CPF and cash usedThe model uses CPF OA savings above the amount kept aside, then cash. Any amount left over is shown as extra money needed.This is a simplified estimate. Actual payment timing and CPF rules can differ.
Rent and monthly payment after rentRent after set-aside is the number of rooms rented times rent per room, less the amount set aside for empty rooms and upkeep. Monthly payment after rent is the loan payment minus this rent estimate.Other household costs are not included. Rent may be lower or absent if a room is empty.
Cash after housingRent after set-aside, minus any loan payment not covered by CPF OA. During the BTO wait, the entered housing cost is also subtracted.A positive amount is left after these modeled costs; a negative amount means more cash is needed. Other bills are excluded.
Monthly spare-cash differenceEach month, the model compares cash after housing for the option with cash after housing for the BTO. Any difference is carried forward using the selected investment return.A positive amount means more cash is left for this option in the model; a negative amount means more is left for BTO. Returns are not guaranteed.
What extra cash might grow to if investedAn example of what extra upfront cash used for resale might grow to if invested at the selected yearly return for the selected period.This is a hypothetical example, not a promised return. CPF savings are shown separately.
Estimated CPF savings and interestThe model starts with CPF OA after upfront use, adds estimated monthly CPF and interest, then subtracts loan payments made from OA above the amount kept aside.This is simplified. It excludes extra CPF interest and some CPF withdrawal rules.
Estimated flat value after loanEstimated flat value minus the loan still owed. The flat value changes by the selected yearly amount over the modeled period.Future flat prices are unknown. This estimate excludes selling costs, CPF balances, rent, and other savings.
Total savings and assetsA full picture would include cash and investments, CPF savings, flat value, loans, other debts, and costs if selling.This calculator does not combine all of these at the same date, so it does not provide one total wealth score.

How to read the money estimates

The investment estimate is one possible use for spare cash, not a guaranteed return. CPF savings are tracked separately. The monthly cash estimate includes loan payments, rent, and any housing cost entered for the BTO wait, but leaves out many other costs.

Why there is no single total score

A complete comparison would need cash and investments, CPF savings, flat value, loan balance, other debts, and costs at the same future date. The options have different purchase and sale dates, so this calculator keeps the estimates separate instead.

The buyer's stamp duty estimate uses current home-purchase rates and assumes no additional buyer's stamp duty. Actual HDB loan approval, grants, flat valuation, remaining lease, rent, and rules may change the result. This tool is only a planning estimate.
