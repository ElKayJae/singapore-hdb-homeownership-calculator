Opportunity cost & home equity

Separate components, not a total-net-worth score

Three separate measures: the BTO's preserved upfront funds are assumed invested at the start; the monthly cash advantage is invested or funded at each month-end; home equity is property value less loan. Do not add either investment alternative to equity as another loss.

Calculation guide

How each result is derived
Definitions and formulas used in the calculator outputs
Output Calculation How to read it
Loan and mortgage payment Loan = min(price × LTV, payment-cap loan). Payment = L × i ÷ [1 − (1 + i)−n], where L is loan, i = annual rate (%) ÷ 100 ÷ 12, and n is term in months. The assessment payment is for the affordability cap; the actual-rate payment is the modeled cash payment.
Upfront funds Price − modeled loan − grant + residential BSD. Excludes legal fees, COV, renovation, furnishing and moving costs.
OA and cash used upfront Use starting OA above the selected buffer first, then starting cash. Any remaining amount is the funding gap. Shows the funding source assumed for the OA-interest calculation. BTO payments are treated as paid upfront, not staged.
Net rent and housing cost Net rent = tenants × gross rent per room × (1 − reserve (%) ÷ 100). Net housing cost = actual mortgage − net rent. Positive cost difference (resale − BTO) means resale costs more per mortgage-active month.
Monthly cash after housing Monthly cash flow = reserve-adjusted rent − mortgage cash top-up after OA withdrawals. For BTO before keys, subtract the entered current housing cost instead. Positive cash flow can be invested; negative cash flow is a cash requirement. The selected OA buffer is preserved.
Monthly investable difference For each month t, dt = option cash flow − BTO cash flow. Future value updates as Vt = Vt−1 × (1 + i) + dt, where i = annual return (%) ÷ 100 ÷ 12. Positive means the resale path has more monthly cash to invest; negative means the BTO path has more. Includes BTO-wait housing cost entered above.
BTO cash investment value Extra cash used by resale vs BTO × [1 + investment return (%) ÷ 100]years. Only extra cash is assigned the investment return; extra OA use is reflected in the CPF interest comparison.
CPF OA interest Start with OA after upfront use; add monthly OA contributions, pay mortgage from OA above the preserved buffer, then apply monthly base interest. Interest lost = BTO interest earned − option interest earned. Positive means the option earns less OA interest than BTO. Includes different mortgage start dates; monthly approximation, excluding extra CPF interest and staged payments.
Projected home equity Home value = price × [1 + annual property change (%) ÷ 100]ownership years. Gross equity = projected home value − remaining mortgage. Resale ownership years use the selected horizon. BTO ownership starts after the key wait; before keys, equity is not modeled.
Total net worth Cash and investments + CPF balances + property value − outstanding loans − other debts and sale costs. The calculator does not combine all these items at one calendar date. Do not subtract opportunity cost again from home equity.

Reading the investment values

Upfront investment value applies only to extra cash used by resale; OA used is tracked separately through OA interest. Monthly cash advantage includes OA-funded mortgage payments, rent, cash top-ups and the entered BTO-wait housing cost. The OA estimate uses monthly contributions and base interest; it excludes extra CPF interest, staged upfront payments and a full common-date CPF forecast.

Net worth accounting

At a common date, total net worth includes cash and investments, CPF balances, property value, less the outstanding loan, other debts and transaction costs. Principal repayment builds home equity. Monthly cash-flow accumulation includes the BTO-wait housing cost you enter, but not other living costs. The OA-interest comparison is a simplified estimate, not a full CPF balance forecast.

BSD uses current residential tier assumptions and assumes no ABSD. The loan cap uses the income-based payment limit at the assessment rate. Actual HFE, grant eligibility, valuation, lease, town-level rent and HDB rules control. This is an editable planning model, not financial advice or a loan approval.
